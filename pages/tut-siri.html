Use Siri Shortcuts

Add bills and log payments hands-free by asking Siri — the app stays in your pocket the whole time.

⏱ 4 min read Intermediate

What you'll learn

  • check_circle Add a bill using Siri by voice
  • check_circle Log a payment without touching your phone
  • check_circle Set up a recurring bill via Siri in one phrase
1

Make sure Siri is enabled

Go to Settings, then Siri & Search, and confirm Siri is turned on. You can also enable "Listen for Hey Siri" here for fully hands-free use.

settings Add your screenshot here
2

Activate Siri

Press the Side button or say "Hey Siri" to open Siri. Wait for the listening indicator before speaking.

mic Add your screenshot here
3

Say "Add a bill in Bills Keeper"

Siri recognises the RemindPay app and opens the bill creation flow directly inside it — you can do this entirely by voice.

record_voice_over Add your screenshot here
4

Speak the bill name when prompted

Siri will ask for the bill name. Speak it clearly, then follow the prompts to enter the amount and due date.

edit_note Add your screenshot here
5

Confirm and save

Siri saves the bill instantly. No need to open the app at all. The bill appears on your home screen the next time you launch RemindPay.

task_alt Add your screenshot here
lightbulb
Pro Tip

You can also say "Add a recurring bill in Bills Keeper" for subscriptions that repeat every month. Siri handles the repeat schedule for you.
