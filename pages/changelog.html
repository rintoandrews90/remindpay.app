3.4.31 3.4.31
October 28, 2026 Latest

Reminders, set up your way

  • notifications_active

    Reminders Set Up Your Way — The first-run screen now asks when you want to hear about a bill, instead of only asking whether notifications may be turned on.

  • schedule

    Pick Your Lead Time — On the due date, a day before, three days, a week or two weeks ahead. Whichever you choose becomes the starting point for every new bill.

  • save

    Your Choices Are Kept — Set the hour reminders arrive, and both answers are saved even if you decide not to turn notifications on.

3.4.30 3.4.30
October 22, 2026

Income reads as received

  • savings

    Income Now Says Received — Money arriving is not money paid, so income entries read Received and Not Received instead of Paid and Unpaid — on Home, in your lists, on the calendar and on the bill itself, including every occurrence of a recurring salary.

  • forward_to_inbox

    Feedback Straight to Support — The Feedback row in Settings now opens your mail app addressed to our support team, with your app version and iOS version already filled in.

  • translate

    Translated Everywhere — A bill status that stayed in English whatever your language is now translated.

3.4.29 3.4.29
October 20, 2026 New feature

A currency for every bill

  • currency_exchange

    A Currency for Every Bill — Each bill can now be saved in the currency you actually pay it in, chosen from the same list as your display currency.

  • compare_arrows

    See Both at Once — A bill in another currency shows its own amount with the equivalent in yours right beside it, on the bill, in your lists and on Home.

  • functions

    Totals That Add Up — Summaries, charts and the Apple Watch app convert every bill into your currency before adding, so mixed-currency totals come out right.
