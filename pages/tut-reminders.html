Set up reminders

Choose exactly when and how far ahead RemindPay alerts you before a bill is due.

⏱ 3 min read Beginner

What you'll learn

  • check_circle Set your daily reminder time
  • check_circle Choose advance notice from 1 day to 2 weeks
  • check_circle Confirm notifications are working correctly
1

Open Settings

Tap the gear icon at the top left of the home screen to open the Settings page. This is where all your notification preferences live.

settings Add your screenshot here
2

Set your reminder time

Choose the time of day you want notifications to arrive. This applies to all your bills and warranty alerts.

schedule Add your screenshot here
3

Choose advance notice

Pick how far ahead you want to be reminded: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, or 2 weeks before the due date. Earlier notice gives you more time to act.

notifications_active Add your screenshot here
4

Check notification permissions

Tap Permissions, then Notifications, and confirm Allow Notifications is turned on. Without this, reminders will not reach you.

verified_user Add your screenshot here
lightbulb
Pro Tip

Pick a time you are usually free, like morning coffee or lunchtime, so you will actually see and act on the reminder.
