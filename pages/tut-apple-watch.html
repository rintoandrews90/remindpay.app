Set up Apple Watch

Install Bills Keeper on your Apple Watch and check upcoming bills right from your wrist.

⏱ 3 min read Beginner

What you'll learn

  • check_circle Install Bills Keeper on your Apple Watch
  • check_circle Browse your upcoming bills from your wrist
  • check_circle Add the Bills Keeper complication to your watch face
1

Open the Watch app on your iPhone

Launch the Watch app on your iPhone. This is where you manage everything installed on your Apple Watch.

watch Add your screenshot here
2

Find Bills Keeper under Available Apps

Scroll down past your installed apps to the Available Apps section and locate Bills Keeper in the list.

search Add your screenshot here
3

Tap Install

Tap Install next to Bills Keeper and wait a moment. The app syncs to your Apple Watch within about a minute.

download Add your screenshot here
4

Open Bills Keeper on your Watch

Once installed, open Bills Keeper on your Apple Watch. Your bills sync automatically. Tap any bill to see its details.

task_alt Add your screenshot here
lightbulb
Pro Tip

Add the Bills Keeper complication to your watch face for an at-a-glance view of your next due bill without opening the app.
